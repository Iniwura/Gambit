# Visual direction and reference library

GAMBIT: Sealed Orders. Original premium sci-fi tactical arena with readable stylized/top-down 2D world and strong operator identity. It must feel like a finished tactical videogame, not a web3 dashboard or generic AI interface.

Engineering branch freezes final graphics until gameplay, architecture and responsive functional acceptance are complete. Do not casually merge a visual overhaul.

## Owner-approved quality references
- Lurais (Framer): https://lurais.framer.website/
- Verris (Framer): https://verris.framer.website/
- Linear and Apple Notes: restrained hierarchy, precise spacing, purposeful controls, clear states, low visual noise.
- Among Us: accessible character readability/social clarity; do not copy IP/art/identity.
- Tactical Breach Wizards: readable tactical choices and consequence.
- Gears Tactics / XCOM: Chimera Squad: squad identity and staged combat presentation.
- Into the Breach: outcome clarity and tactical legibility.
- Vesperfall: art/world/HUD benchmark cited in repository README.

These are quality benchmarks, not templates to clone. GAMBIT needs original art and distinct visual language.

## Visual intent
Coherent reactor arena; deliberate camera framing; readable sectors; meaningful objective feedback; three distinct fighter silhouettes; strong HUD hierarchy (match, round, pressure, actor, one primary action); purpose-built controls rather than generic rectangles; selected/hover/pressed/disabled/loading/error states; compact accessible menus; consistent typography/grid/spacing/icon language; meaningful state-driven animation; rights-cleared sound. Never visually reveal secret plans where only aggregate results are authorized.

## R36 separate visual track
Branch design/arena-visual-track contains R36 disclosure/UX checkpoint and visual candidate. It is separate, not merged or approved production art. Inspect README, docs, manifests and source archives before reuse. Verify licenses, provenance, editable formats, dependencies, responsive behavior and whether files are in Git or external. README reports three animated textured operator models and 3D Arena/Academy/BLACKSITE/studio candidate, local progression and disclosure UX tested offline. Source archives were supplied separately; not asserted to be in this engineering branch.

## Asset standards
Original/properly licensed only. Maintain consistent camera, scale, palette, lighting, line weight, anatomy, silhouettes and pivots across six approved fighters. Keep editable sources and export pipeline. Document atlas names/dimensions/anchors. Separate environments, props, characters, UI, VFX and audio. No unlicensed references, copied identity, watermarks or copyrighted music.
