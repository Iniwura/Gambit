## Design principle
The world is the interface. One primary action per state, only relevant tactical choices visible. No cards around the whole world, dashboard navigation, decorative status chips, repetitive labels or permanent grid of controls. Complexity lives in the tactics, not the interface. Study Tactical Breach Wizards' editable turn drafts and undo-before-commit, not its opponent previews (our rival orders remain confidential): https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/tactical-breach-wizards-shows-how-strategy-games-can-tickle-the-funny-bone

## Interaction design
- Boot -> a single Play action. HQ -> simple four-item mode menu over full-bleed scene. No website-style hero copy or feature tiles. Settings contextual. Training is available without wallet.
- Arena -> Create or Join/Resume. Invitation, participant and wallet details contextual to that action, not permanent HUD.
- Arena 1600×900: game scene occupies top 675px under small floating HUD. At y24..76, logo/mode left, score and five round markers center, round/state right. Preserve readable characters; deadlines and actual rival seal flags are relevant status, not decorative labels.
- At y675..900, bottom contextual action deck. x36..307: three role selectors with unique icons, completed indicators. x370..860: selected role and ONE decision prompt. x888..1556: three tactile action cards (Strike, Guard, Flank) only. Each selection updates private draft and advances opening → predicted rival opening → conditional response. Advance to next incomplete role; player can select an existing role or go back to edit. Explicitly distinguish prediction from actual enemy intelligence. Do not silently auto-select tactical orders. Keep full canonical nine inputs.
- When all three roles complete, show compact squad check: formation HOLD/PIVOT/FEINT and optional GAMBIT. Sector selection opens only when invoked. GAMBIT activation public, sector private; actual availability and spent status from contract. Edit orders until seal; no edit after accepted seal. Show exact transaction review, wallet actor/network/fee/action/round/calldata binding and actual explicit confirmation. No unauthorized signing, automatic retries or invented readiness.
- Waiting: real encryption/receipt/peer/KMS states, elapsed time if known, no fictional progress. Keep arena visible, allow safe return and recover public state. Volatile plaintext/ciphertext not persisted. Pending/unknown-dispatch/expiry flow remains fail-closed.
- Resolution: show only aggregate authenticated points in encrypted Arena; no individual combat outcomes/hidden formation. Local practice may animate actual per-role outcomes. Result and debrief prioritize score/outcome, rematch; proof detail optional.
- Blacksite: physical vault scene; only four glyph slots, three approaches, current attempts/heat and clues. Warden creation separate, preserve actual competitive availability. Commander: verified level/XP/history only. No fictional achievements.
- Mobile 390×844: small top HUD, scene framing y62..431, current prompt at y463..541, three large action cards at y575..678, role selectors y711..783. All other states must be redesigned to follow this grammar. Landscape and full responsive/state variants pending.
- Proposed visual tokens: near-black #10191E, dark #19272D, surface #293B40, edge #577172, bone #F3EDDB, muted #B7C4C0, primary warm gold #F7D174, ally #88D8D2, rival #E89D78. Roboto Condensed Bold display, Inter controls. Original two opposing wedge mark. Reusable icon silhouettes for roles and moves. No font files bundled.
- Motion: tactile selection tick, short card transition, role focus/pulse, visible authenticated score response, reduce-motion alternative. No unnecessary animations. Sound and final art still need actual review.
