# Approved GAMBIT design benchmarks

Owner approved 25 November 2026. Approval covers the reference games, not final designs or implementation.

- Primary overall art direction: Tactical Breach Wizards — stylized isometric stages, readable characters and ability interactions, coherent world.
- Aspirational environment, lighting and cinematic quality: Gears Tactics — study material/lighting hierarchy and deliberate camera staging; do not promise AAA fidelity.
- Character identity and squad presentation: XCOM: Chimera Squad — three strongly differentiated original characters, meaningful roles, readable silhouettes.
- Gameplay clarity and information hierarchy: Into the Breach — show only actionable information; teach through clear game states, no unnecessary HUD ornament.

Original assets only; no copying game logos, character art, maps, screenshots as production art, or proprietary interface assets. Keep original identity and exact GAMBIT mechanics. Design in [Figma Design Master](https://www.figma.com/design/BFKGcmON6Gh6KQlRLI35Po); artwork, typography, logo, screen states, controls, animation, audio and responsive variants must receive review before visual implementation. Functional branch remains independent. No fake competitive data or private opponent tactics; only public aggregate outcomes in encrypted matches.

Review gate: all game journeys from loader to debrief, all four modes, error/recovery states, accessible and responsive controls, component/asset specification, character/arena production sheets and linked prototype. Reference approval is not design lock.
