## Quality disposition
This is a useful technical asset trial, not a 90+ game. The selected models are visibly mechanical and have limited personality; the arena retains a sparse kit-like diorama appearance, the HUD is not final, and full-game audiovisual production and all four pillars' design preview are incomplete. Do not expand or integrate this art merely because import works. The approved game benchmarks, R01–R04 rejections and owner design-lock gate remain in force. Preserve the functional branch. First decide on a cohesive character family and environment composition that genuinely meet the art benchmark, then repeat visual review with actual asset imagery. No production code changed, no merges or transactions.
